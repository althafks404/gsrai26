About "Global Conference on Sustainable Research and AI Driven Innovation"

GCSRAI 26

The Conference is dedicated to advancing cutting-edge research at the intersection of sustainability, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. The conference brings together researchers, academicians, industry experts, policymakers, and innovators from around the world to exchange ideas, share breakthroughs, and foster interdisciplinary collaboration.

Key Highlights

  • International keynote speakers and expert panels

  • Peer-reviewed research paper presentations

  • Focus on sustainability, AI, and emerging technologies

  • Industry–academia collaboration opportunities

  • Platform for young researchers and innovators

  • Networking with global experts and institutions
