Biography

Dr. Sherimon P.C. serves as Dean, Faculty of Computer Science, Arab Open University, Muscat, Sultanate of Oman. He holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science along with two Master's Degrees in Computer Science and Information Technology.

He previously served as Lecturer at the School of Computer Sciences, Mahatma Gandhi University, India and as Head of Section in the Department of Information Technology at Higher College of Technology, Muscat.

With more than 23 years of teaching, research and administrative experience, Dr. Sherimon has made significant contributions to computer science education, research, innovation and academic leadership across multiple institutions.
