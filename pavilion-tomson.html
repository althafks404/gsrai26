Electronics • Robotics • IoT • Product Development

Tomson Electronics Pvt. Ltd. is a Kochi-based electronics and technology company with over 38 years of experience in supplying electronic components, robotics hardware, IoT solutions, and engineering products.

Serving customers across India, the company supports innovation through component distribution, electronics manufacturing services, product development, and STEM education initiatives, helping transform ideas into market-ready solutions.

About the Company

Tomson Electronics has established itself as one of India's trusted suppliers of electronic components, development boards, sensors, robotics kits, embedded systems hardware, and engineering tools.

The company works closely with students, startups, educational institutions, researchers, and industries by providing access to cutting-edge technologies and innovative electronic solutions.

Through its strong commitment to technology education and product development, Tomson Electronics continues to support India's growing ecosystem of innovators, makers, engineers, and entrepreneurs.
