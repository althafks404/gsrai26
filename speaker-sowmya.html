Biography

Dr. Sowmya V is currently an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham, Coimbatore. She previously served as Assistant Professor at the Center for Computational Engineering and Networking (CEN), Amrita Coimbatore.

She completed her M.Tech in Remote Sensing and Wireless Sensor Networks from Amrita School of Engineering, Coimbatore and earned her Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence for Natural Scene Analysis from Amrita Vishwa Vidyapeetham.

Her doctoral research focused on Artificial Intelligence for Computer Vision, Healthcare Data Analysis and Remote Sensing Data Analysis, which aligns closely with her current research interests.
