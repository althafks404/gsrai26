Biography

Dr. Vipinraj Sugathan is an accomplished materials scientist and researcher at the Centre for Analysis and Synthesis, Lund University, Sweden.

He earned a Joint Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IIT Bombay) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore.

His research focuses on advanced nanomaterials, particularly perovskite-based materials for next-generation optoelectronic devices and renewable energy applications.

He has established a strong international research profile through his contributions to solar energy technologies, flexible electronics, and advanced photonic devices.
