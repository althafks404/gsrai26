Biography

Dr. Nivea Thomas is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at the National Institute of Technology (NIT) Warangal, India.

She holds a Ph.D. in Construction Management from the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Delhi, an M.Tech. in Construction Engineering and Management from TKM College of Engineering, Kerala, and a B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from Amal Jyothi College of Engineering, Kerala.

Her research interests encompass construction project management, infrastructure management and policy, project performance evaluation, air transport management, and regional aviation.

Dr. Thomas has published extensively in reputed international journals and conference proceedings, with notable contributions in construction management and regional airport development.

She has authored book chapters, served as a reviewer for leading international journals and conferences, and delivered invited lectures on infrastructure management and regional airport sustainability.
