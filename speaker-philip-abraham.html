Professional Profile

Mr. Philip Abraham is an accomplished entrepreneur, technology leader, and business strategist with more than three decades of experience in international business, software development, and technology-driven innovation.

He is the Founder and CEO of Reubro International, a global technology company headquartered in Kochi, India, established in 1996.

His leadership combines technical expertise, global business understanding, and strategic vision to drive innovation and sustainable growth.

Founder & CEO - Reubro International

Under his leadership, Reubro International has grown into a trusted technology partner for organizations across multiple countries.

The company specializes in software development, mobile applications, web platforms, digital transformation initiatives, data analytics, and AI-enabled solutions.

Reubro delivers end-to-end product development services that help organizations accelerate innovation and improve business performance.
