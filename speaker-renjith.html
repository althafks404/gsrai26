Biography

Dr. V.R. Renjith received his Ph.D. in Industrial Safety from Cochin University of Science and Technology (CUSAT) in 2011. He obtained his Master's Degree in Industrial Safety from NIT Trichy, securing First Rank and a Gold Medal.

He has more than 24 years of teaching experience in the fields of Safety Engineering and Fire Engineering. In addition to teaching, he actively contributes as a researcher, consultant, trainer and quiz master in safety-related disciplines.

His academic and professional work focuses on industrial safety, fire protection, process safety management, risk assessment and sustainable safety engineering.
