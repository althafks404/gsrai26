NeST Group

Engineering • Manufacturing • Digital Transformation • Innovation

NeST Group is a Kochi-headquartered global conglomerate with business interests spanning technology, engineering, electronics manufacturing, digital transformation, education, infrastructure, and consumer products.

Established in 1990, the Group operates across more than 30 international locations and is recognized for delivering innovative engineering, manufacturing, and digital solutions under its vision of "Develop and Make in India for the World."

About NeST Group

NeST Group has grown into one of India's leading technology-driven enterprises with a strong presence in engineering services, manufacturing, software development, system integration, education, and infrastructure solutions.

With operations spanning multiple countries, the Group supports customers worldwide through innovation, quality, and advanced engineering capabilities.

SFO Technologies – Flagship Enterprise

SFO Technologies is the flagship engineering and manufacturing company of NeST Group. It provides end-to-end product design, electronics manufacturing, software development, product engineering, and system integration services.
