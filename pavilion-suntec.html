About the Company

SunTec Business Solutions is one of India's leading global enterprise software companies, enabling organizations to transform customer experiences, optimize business operations, and accelerate digital innovation.

SunTec Xelerate Platform

SunTec's flagship platform, SunTec Xelerate, enables enterprises to simplify pricing, billing, taxation, partner settlement, and customer engagement processes through a unified digital business platform.

The platform empowers organizations to improve operational efficiency, accelerate innovation, and create personalized customer experiences across multiple industries.
