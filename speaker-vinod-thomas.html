Professional Profile

Mr. Vinod Thomas is a distinguished technology entrepreneur, business strategist, and former Indian Police Service (IPS) officer.

A 1993-batch IPS officer of the Kerala cadre, he successfully transitioned from public service into leadership roles in technology, innovation, and business transformation.

His unique career journey combines governance, organizational leadership, technology innovation, and strategic decision-making.

Founder & CEO - Deep Blue Research

Mr. Thomas is the Founder and CEO of Deep Blue Research, an AI-focused innovation company dedicated to applying Artificial Intelligence, advanced analytics, and data-driven decision-making to solve complex business and societal challenges.

Under his leadership, Deep Blue Research focuses on building practical AI solutions that drive innovation, productivity, and sustainable growth.
