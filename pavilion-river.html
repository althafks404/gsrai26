River Mobility Pvt. Ltd.

Electric Mobility • Sustainable Transportation • EV Innovation

River Mobility Pvt. Ltd. is an innovative electric vehicle company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. Founded in 2020, River develops utility-focused electric two-wheelers designed to enhance everyday mobility through sustainability, performance, and smart design.

With its award-winning River Indie electric scooter, the company is emerging as a leading force in India's rapidly growing electric mobility ecosystem.

About the Company

River Mobility is redefining urban transportation by creating practical electric vehicles built for everyday users. The company's products combine advanced engineering, smart technology, utility-focused design, and sustainability.

River aims to make electric mobility accessible and useful for a broad range of consumers while contributing to a cleaner, greener future through innovative EV solutions.

River Indie

River Indie is the company's flagship electric scooter, designed to deliver practicality, performance, and versatility for everyday commuting. Its innovative design, storage capacity, riding comfort, and smart engineering have earned recognition within India's EV industry.
