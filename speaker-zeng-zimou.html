Biography

Mr. Zeng Zimou is a Robotics Engineer, technology entrepreneur, and Founder of Rosen Bridge Technology, operating across Singapore and China.

He specializes in robotics, artificial intelligence, intelligent automation systems, and advanced technology solutions that bridge research and industrial applications.

Through Rosen Bridge Technology, he has been actively involved in developing innovative robotics solutions that integrate AI technologies to improve efficiency, automation, and decision-making capabilities.

His work focuses on applying artificial intelligence to real-world robotic systems, enabling smarter and more adaptive machines for future industries.
