Biography

Dr. Naveen Joseph is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Geospatial and Earth Sciences at Radford University, Virginia, USA.

He obtained his Ph.D. in Environmental Hydrology and Water Resources from the University of Melbourne, Australia, after completing his M.Tech. in Hydraulics and Water Resources Engineering from IIT Madras and B.Tech. in Civil Engineering from TKM College of Engineering, Kerala.

Prior to joining Radford University, he served as Postdoctoral Research Fellow and Interim Director at the Idaho Water Resources Research Institute, University of Idaho, USA.

His research focuses on sustainable water management, environmental resilience, geospatial analysis, climate adaptation and environmental health.
